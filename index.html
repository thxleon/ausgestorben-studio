Ausgestorben Studio

Ausgestorben Studio is a small personal tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @ausgestorbenodernicht.

The creator produces short educational videos about prehistoric animals, living fossils and species that return to the wild. Ausgestorben Studio uploads these finished videos to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts (TikTok inbox) via the TikTok Content Posting API. The creator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account once (Login with TikTok, scope video.upload).
  2. A finished MP4 video is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox.
  3. The creator opens the inbox notification in TikTok, edits the caption if needed and posts it manually.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not post anything without the creator's review.
